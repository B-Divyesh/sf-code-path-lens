The method / 02

Evidence before explanation.

Code Path Lens does not answer questions about your code. It assembles the small, reproducible body of evidence you need to answer them yourself.

Parse locally

Tree-sitter adapters index declarations and calls in Rust, TypeScript/JavaScript, Python, and Go while respecting .gitignore and generated-code markers.

Cut to a bound

A bidirectional walk follows callees and callers only as far as your depth and node budgets allow. The output says when it was truncated.

Carry the proof

HTML, JSON, and DOT outputs preserve file, line, excerpt, edge kind, and resolution status. Share the slice, not the repository.
